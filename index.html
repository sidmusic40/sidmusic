About S.I.D

The person behind the beats.

Music · Worship · Impact

I'm S.I.D — a producer who believes a beat can carry more than a bounce; it can carry purpose. Everything you hear on this site comes from one small studio, one pair of headphones, and a conviction that music inspires change.

From worship leaders who need a sound for Sunday, to rappers who want truth in their trap — I build beats that serve the message, not override it. Mix-ready, tagged by genre and BPM, and made to be rapped, sung, and worshipped on.
